This is Madison,
the SLP behind
Social House Therapy.

For Madison Jeffery, CCC-SLP, speech therapy has always been about more than words. As a child, she worked with a speech-language pathologist who helped her connect more meaningfully with the people around her. That experience shaped her career and the belief behind Social House Therapy: communication begins with feeling seen, understood, and supported.

Madison previously worked in the Kyrene School District, so she knows the schools Tempe families navigate. We coordinate with teachers and IEP teams in the Tempe Elementary, Kyrene, and Tempe Union districts.

What we help Tempe kids with.

01

Early Communication & Late Talkers

Fewer words, gestures, sounds, or communication attempts than expected for their age.

 02

Autism & Social Communication

Play, connection, interaction, gestalt language development, and authentic communication.

 03

Speech Sound Disorders

Support for speech that is unclear or difficult to understand due to sound errors or motor-planning and coordination challenges.

 04

Understanding & Using Language

Following directions, answering questions, building sentences, and telling stories.

 05

Stuttering & Fluency

Building confidence and ease with less pressure around speaking.

 06

Pediatric Feeding & Oral-Motor

Support for chewing, drinking, food textures, oral-motor development, and safer, calmer, more confident mealtime skills.

 07

AAC Support

Communication boards, speech-generating devices, signs, and coaching for children and their communication partners.

Yes. We offer three options: in-home in Tempe and nearby areas; on-site at ABA and childcare centers, subject to facility approval; and telehealth statewide across Arizona. Some families use a combination of in-home and telehealth services when clinically appropriate and scheduling allows.

You are welcome to contact us directly. A medical or developmental diagnosis is not required to schedule a consultation or speech-language evaluation. We are currently a private-pay practice, and no referral is needed for private-pay clients.

When everyone is working toward the same goals, your child hears the same expectations at home, at school, and in session.
Right now, every session is provided by a licensed speech-language pathologist. As our team grows, sessions may also be provided by a speech-language pathology assistant or a student clinician, always under the supervision of a certified SLP. That’s okay. Children do not need to sit at a table or participate in a specific way for therapy to be valuable. We use movement, play, preferred activities, and your child’s interests to build connection and work toward meaningful goals.
We believe different brains and communication styles belong. Our goal is not to make a child appear more typical. We do not target harmless stimming, scripting, or autistic traits simply because they look different.
