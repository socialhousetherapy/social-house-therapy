Answers for Tempe families.

Yes. We offer three options: in-home in Tempe and nearby areas; on-site at ABA and childcare centers, subject to facility approval; and telehealth statewide across Arizona. Some families use a combination of in-home and telehealth services when clinically appropriate and scheduling allows.
Start with a free 15-minute consultation. We’ll listen to your concerns, answer initial questions, and recommend the most appropriate next step. If an evaluation is recommended, we’ll help you schedule it. If your child completed a comprehensive evaluation within the last six months, we can review it to determine whether it meets current clinical and documentation needs.
You are welcome to contact us directly. A medical or developmental diagnosis is not required to schedule a consultation or speech-language evaluation. We are currently a private-pay practice, and no referral is needed for private-pay clients.
Evaluation and therapy costs are reviewed with you before scheduling, so you know exactly what to expect. Evaluations are offered at one flat rate that includes the session, written report, and follow-up consultation. See our pricing section for current therapy rates and payment options.
At this time, we are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We accept Arizona ESA funding, HSA and FSA funds, and all major debit and credit cards, and we can provide a superbill that you may submit to your insurance company for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Insurance participation is coming soon for select locations and service settings.
Yes, and we love to. With your written permission, we’re glad to collaborate with teachers and IEP teams in the Tempe Elementary, Kyrene, and Tempe Union districts, along with your child’s pediatrician, occupational and physical therapists, ABA team, and childcare providers. When everyone is working toward the same goals, progress carries over across settings.
That’s okay. Children do not need to sit at a table or participate in a specific way for therapy to be valuable. We use movement, play, preferred activities, and your child’s interests to build connection and work toward meaningful goals.
We believe different brains and communication styles belong. Our goal is not to make a child appear more typical. We do not target harmless stimming, scripting, or autistic traits simply because they look different. Instead, we support communication, autonomy, regulation, participation, and self-advocacy in ways that respect who your child already is.
