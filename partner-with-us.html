Speech therapy during the day, not after it

You already know which children are hard to understand, slow to talk, or struggling at circle time. Their parents know too, and most are stuck between a months-long clinic waitlist and a drive across town with a tired toddler. Our therapists come to you on a consistent weekly schedule and see children where they already are.

Sessions run in a quiet room, or inside the classroom when that serves the goal better. Children are walked to and from. Teachers get one practical strategy they can use the same day. We handle consent, enrollment, scheduling, documentation, and billing.

what a visit looks like
  1. We arrive on our scheduled day and check in at the front desk.
  2. Each child is seen one-on-one, or supported during natural routines like centers and snack.
  3. Teachers get a short handoff: what we worked on, and one thing to try this week.
  4. Parents get a same-day note in plain language.

Speech woven into the day your clients already have

Your families are managing a full therapy schedule, and many are also driving to a separate speech clinic or waiting for one. Bringing speech into your center means both disciplines in one place, from teams that talk to each other.

We schedule around your authorizations so speech never collides with ABA hours, and we collaborate with your BCBAs directly. If a client already uses AAC, supporting it is a particular strength: we build the system into sessions and train your RBTs to model on it, so it gets used through the day rather than only with us. We do not conduct AAC evaluations ourselves, and we will point you toward clinicians who do. We never sell devices and take no commissions.

how we work with your team
  1. Intake includes a conversation with the client's BCBA, not only the parents.
  2. Sessions run on-site in a weekly slot that respects your authorization schedule.
  3. Speech goals and behavior plans are coordinated so the two reinforce each other.
  4. Progress notes go to the family and, with consent, to your clinical team.

Student support services, without hiring an SLP

Public districts have speech services built in. Your families gave that up when they chose you, and some are leaving campus early every week to get it back. A partnership puts a licensed speech-language pathologist on your campus instead of sending them off it.

Sessions are planned with teachers so students never repeatedly miss the same subject, and we coordinate on what carries back into the classroom. Families remain our clients, so your school takes on no clinical liability, no employment relationship, and no billing role.

how it fits your campus

  2. Students are seen in short weekly sessions timed with teachers, not against them.

Getting started

From first call to first session

Most partnerships go from introduction to children being seen in a few weeks. You will not be handed a program to run. We bring the program to you.

A short intro call

Fifteen minutes with our clinical lead about your program, your families, and whether this is a fit. An honest no is a fine outcome. We would rather be useful than persistent.

A walkthrough and a simple agreement

We visit your site, agree on a space and a weekly schedule, and sign a straightforward facility agreement. Credentials and insurance certificates go into your records at this stage.

We introduce the service to your families

You announce it however suits you: a flyer, a newsletter, a mention at pickup. We take it from there, handling interest, consent, intake, and coverage checks with each family directly.

Therapy begins on-site

The same therapist, the same day each week, seeing enrolled children at your location. Consistency is the point: children do best with a familiar face in a familiar place.

An ongoing loop, not a drop-in

Parent updates after each session, collaboration with your team throughout, and regular check-ins with your leadership to make sure the partnership is earning its place.

Private pay
