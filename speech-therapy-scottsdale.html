This is Madison,
the SLP behind
Social House Therapy.

For Madison Jeffery, CCC-SLP, speech therapy has always been about more than words. As a child, she worked with a speech-language pathologist who helped her connect more meaningfully with the people around her. That experience shaped her career and the belief behind Social House Therapy: communication begins with feeling seen, understood, and supported.

Madison grew up in Scottsdale, so she knows the community families call home: the preschools, the parks, the drive across town at 5pm. We coordinate with teachers and IEP teams in the Scottsdale Unified and Paradise Valley Unified districts.

Answers for Scottsdale families.

Yes. We offer three options: in-home in Scottsdale and nearby areas; on-site at ABA and childcare centers, subject to facility approval; and telehealth statewide across Arizona. Some families use a combination of in-home and telehealth services when clinically appropriate and scheduling allows.
Start with a free 15-minute consultation. We’ll listen to your concerns, answer initial questions, and recommend the most appropriate next step. If an evaluation is recommended, we’ll help you schedule it. If your child completed a comprehensive evaluation within the last six months, we can review it to determine whether it meets current clinical and documentation needs.
You are welcome to contact us directly. A medical or developmental diagnosis is not required to schedule a consultation or speech-language evaluation. We are currently a private-pay practice, and no referral is needed for private-pay clients.
Every cost is reviewed with you before anything is scheduled. See the rates above for evaluation and therapy pricing.
At this time, we are a private-pay practice and do not bill insurance directly. We accept Arizona ESA (ClassWallet) funding, HSA and FSA funds, and all major debit and credit cards, and we can provide a superbill that you may submit to your insurance company for possible out-of-network reimbursement.
Yes, and we love to. With your written permission, we’re glad to collaborate with teachers and IEP teams in the Scottsdale Unified and Paradise Valley Unified districts, along with your child’s pediatrician, occupational and physical therapists, ABA team, and childcare providers. When everyone is working toward the same goals, your child hears the same expectations at home, at school, and in session.
Right now, every session is provided by a licensed speech-language pathologist. As our team grows, sessions may also be provided by a speech-language pathology assistant or a student clinician, always under the supervision of a certified SLP. You will know who your child is working with before the first session.
That’s okay. Children do not need to sit at a table or participate in a specific way for therapy to be valuable. We use movement, play, preferred activities, and your child’s interests to build connection and work toward meaningful goals.
We believe different brains and communication styles belong. Our goal is not to make a child appear more typical. We do not target harmless stimming, scripting, or autistic traits simply because they look different. Instead, we support communication, autonomy, regulation, participation, and self-advocacy in ways that respect who your child already is.
