This is Madison,
the SLP behind
Social House Therapy.

For Madison Jeffery, CCC-SLP, speech therapy has always been about more than words. As a child, she worked with a speech-language pathologist who helped her connect more meaningfully with the people around her. That experience shaped her career and the belief behind Social House Therapy: communication begins with feeling seen, understood, and supported.

She holds degrees from Arizona State University and Northern Arizona University, and works with late talkers, speech sound and language delays, stuttering, feeding, AAC, and autism.

Early Communication & Late Talkers

Fewer words, gestures, sounds, or communication attempts than expected for their age.

 02

Autism & Social Communication

Play, connection, interaction, echolalia and gestalt language, and authentic communication.

 03

Speech Sound Disorders

Support for speech that is unclear or difficult to understand due to sound errors or motor-planning and coordination challenges.

 04

Understanding & Using Language

Following directions, answering questions, building sentences, and telling stories.

 05

Stuttering & Fluency

Building confidence and ease with less pressure around speaking.

 06

Pediatric Feeding & Oral-Motor

Support for chewing, drinking, food textures, oral-motor development, and safer, calmer, more confident mealtime skills.

 07

AAC Support

Communication boards, speech-generating devices, signs, and coaching for children and their communication partners.
